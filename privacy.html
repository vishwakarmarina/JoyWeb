Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

JoyFlow is built and maintained by an independent developer. This page explains, plainly, what data JoyFlow accesses, why, and — most importantly — what happens to it.

The short version

JoyFlow does not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. Everything JoyFlow reads from your device stays on your device, stored locally, and is never transmitted to any server controlled by us or any third party — with one narrow exception described below for the AI Assistant feature.

Permissions JoyFlow uses, and why

Notification access

Powers the Messages and Activity Feed screens, which show a local copy of your recent notifications inside JoyFlow for quick reference. This data is read and stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Usage access (screen time)

Powers the Screen Time feature, which shows how long you've used your phone today and categorizes it as Good, Moderate, or High. This calculation happens entirely on your device. No usage data ever leaves your phone.

Installed apps list

Powers the "My Apps" shortcuts feature, letting you pin up to five apps to your JoyFlow home screen for quick launching. JoyFlow reads your installed app list only to display it in the app picker — this list is never sent anywhere.

Internet access

Used only when you actively use the AI Assistant feature. Your message is sent securely to power the AI response, and nothing else — no notifications, no usage stats, no app list, no personal files — is ever included in that request.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't use advertising SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties.
  • We don't require an account, email, or phone number to use JoyFlow.

Data storage

All app data — tasks, notes, planner events, settings, and cached notifications — is stored locally on your device using standard Android app storage. Uninstalling JoyFlow removes all of this data permanently.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of JoyFlow after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how JoyFlow handles data can be sent through the contact details listed on the JoyFlow download page.
